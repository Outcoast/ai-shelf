## Step 3: the sentence

Phrases the AIs repeated:
- Timeless and Maelove: "budget dupe for SkinCeuticals" (Gemini), "Best Luxury Dupe" (Google AI Mode)
- Timeless: "Wirecutter's budget pick" (Perplexity, every run from run 2 on)
- Timeless, Maelove: "widely considered the overall best" (Gemini)

The winning pattern: a comparison to a famous expensive product + a named editorial pick. Both are written by someone else, and both are short enough to quote.

For a brand that isn't on the shelf yet, a one-liner in this shape would be: "the {price} alternative to {famous product}, {checkable spec}". It only works if it's true and provable.
