# Source playbook

What to do for each type of source the AIs cite. Always lead with the one-liner and the proof.

## Editorial lists and review sites (Wirecutter, Allure, Byrdie, TODAY, Good Housekeeping...)

- Find the exact article the AI cites, and the writer. Check when it was last updated (most "best of" lists get refreshed).
- Pitch: 4-5 lines. What it is, the one-liner, the proof (test, %, price), why it fits that specific list, an offer to send samples.
- Ask to be considered for the next update. Never ask for a link or a placement in exchange for money unless it's a disclosed ad.

Pitch template:
```
Subject: {product} for your "{article title}" update

Hi {name},
Your {article} is cited every time I ask ChatGPT and Perplexity about {category}.
{product} is {one-liner}. {proof in 1 sentence}.
Happy to send samples for your next update, no strings.
{signature}
```

## YouTube and TikTok creators (dermatologists, reviewers)

- Find the videos the AIs cite (Google AI Mode cites YouTube a lot).
- Seed product with a short note: what it is, the one-liner, no script, no required talking points.
- Any paid or gifted content must be disclosed by the creator.

## Reddit

- The AIs cite threads where real users compare products.
- Do: answer questions openly as the brand (flair or clear username), share facts, link to proof, take criticism.
- Never: fake accounts, planted "I found this amazing serum" posts, paid upvotes. It gets found, it gets banned, and it poisons the thread the AI reads.

## Retailers (Amazon, Sephora, Ulta, Target, Walmart)

- Same title, same key claims, same one-liner on every listing. Inconsistencies make AIs hedge or skip you.
- Reviews: ask real customers after delivery, through the retailer's allowed programs. Never buy or incentivize positive reviews.

## Brand site

- The AI reads it when it checks facts (stock, price, ingredients). Keep it accurate. ChatGPT recommended a serum as #1 while noting it was sold out: stock status gets read.

## Blogs and small sites

- Some AIs lean on small blogs. Same pitch as editorial, shorter. Prioritize the ones cited by 2+ AIs.
