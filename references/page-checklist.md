# Page checklist (hero product page)

- [ ] Answer block at the top: 2-3 sentences that answer the shopper question, with the one-liner, price, key spec, and who it's for
- [ ] The one-liner appears word for word, once, in plain text (not only inside an image)
- [ ] Key specs as text: active ingredient and %, size, price, skin type, what it's for
- [ ] FAQ with the real shopper questions as headings, 2-3 sentence answers
- [ ] Comparison section if the one-liner compares (factual, no attacks)
- [ ] Proof on the page: test results, awards, press quotes, with where they come from
- [ ] Reviews visible as text on the page
- [ ] Stock and price accurate (AIs read them)
- [ ] Product structured data (schema.org Product with price, availability, reviews) if the site platform supports it
- [ ] Same title, claims and one-liner on Amazon and every retailer listing
- [ ] Page loads without needing JavaScript to show the text (check with "view source" or ask your developer)

Example answer block:
```
{Product} is a {spec} {product type} for {who}, at {price}.
{One-liner}.
{Proof: tested by / rated / picked by}.
```
